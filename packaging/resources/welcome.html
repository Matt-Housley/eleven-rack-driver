Eleven Rack Audio Driver

This installs a user-space Core Audio driver for the Avid Eleven Rack. Your Eleven Rack appears in Audio MIDI Setup and any DAW as an 8-in / 6-out device at 44.1, 48, 88.2 or 96 kHz.

Before you continue, quit any apps that are playing audio or video (Safari, Music, your DAW…). Installing restarts the macOS audio service, which briefly interrupts playback and can leave a playing app stuck.

What gets installed:

  • A Core Audio plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL
  • The Eleven Rack menu-bar app in /Applications
  • A login item so the driver starts automatically

No kernel extension, no system extension, and no “Reduced Security” change. The installer restarts the audio service and needs your administrator password once.
